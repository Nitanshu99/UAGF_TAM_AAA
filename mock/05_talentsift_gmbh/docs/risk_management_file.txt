RISK MANAGEMENT SYSTEM FILE  |  Ref: RMF-TR-v1.2-2026
Pursuant to EU AI Act Article 9 and Annex IV para 5
Approved by: Dr. Miriam Hoffmann, Head of Model Governance  |  Date: 2026-05-10

1. SCOPE
TalentRank v1.2 is a linear text classifier (scikit-learn TF-IDF +
logistic regression) provided by TalentSift GmbH (Hamburg) and deployed
by Nordwind Personal Services AG to pre-screen free-text CVs for
shortlist recommendations. Risk tier: HIGH -- Annex III para 4(a)
(recruitment, screening and filtering of applications).

2. IDENTIFIED RISKS  (Art. 9 para 2(a))
R-001  Discriminatory shortlisting against protected groups        HIGH
R-002  Proxy discrimination via language/vocabulary correlates     HIGH
R-003  Vocabulary drift as job markets and CV conventions change  MEDIUM
R-004  Keyword-stuffing manipulation by applicants                MEDIUM
R-005  Automation bias -- recruiters rubber-stamping the ranking    HIGH
R-006  Residual personal data surviving the PII-stripping step    MEDIUM

3. MITIGATION MEASURES  (Art. 9 para 2(c))
R-001: Quarterly demographic parity and equal-opportunity testing per
       sex, age group and nationality; recalibrated shortlist threshold.
R-002: Token-importance review each release; blocklist of location,
       school-name and language tokens found to proxy protected status.
R-003: Token-distribution PSI monitoring (alert at PSI > 0.2) over the
       top-100 TF-IDF mass; scheduled annual re-training.
R-004: Character-noise and synonym robustness probes at each release;
       duplicate-phrase density cap flagging keyword-stuffed CVs.
R-005: Mandatory recruiter confirmation with reason codes; override
       rate KPI reviewed monthly; no auto-rejection pathway exists.
R-006: Two-stage PII stripper (rule-based + NER) with quarterly leak
       audit on a 200-CV sample.

4. RESIDUAL RISK ASSESSMENT  (Art. 9 para 5)
All residual risks are judged acceptable for the intended purpose under
human-in-the-loop operation. Wrongful non-shortlisting is bounded by the
recruiter review step and the recall-oriented threshold policy.

5. TESTING  (Art. 9 para 6-8)
Release gates: accuracy/F1/AUC on the held-out evaluation set; fairness
suite per protected attribute; char-noise adversarial probe; PII-leak
scan. Results are archived per release in the model registry.

6. REVIEW CYCLE
This file is reviewed semi-annually and at every substantial
modification (Art. 25 assessment attached to each review).
