TalentSift GmbH -- TalentRank v1.2
POST-MARKET MONITORING PLAN  |  Ref: PMM-TR-v1.2-2026
Pursuant to EU AI Act Article 72
Owner: Model Governance Team  |  Effective: 2026-05-15

1. MONITORING SCOPE
Production shortlist recommendations issued by TalentRank v1.2 at all
deployer sites, covering performance, fairness, robustness and human-
oversight indicators throughout the operational lifetime.

2. DATA COLLECTED
Per-screening logs (hashed applicant_id, vacancy family, score,
recommendation, recruiter decision, override flag); quarterly labelled
audit samples of 200 screenings per deployer; token-distribution
statistics per month.

3. INDICATORS AND THRESHOLDS
M-01 Shortlist accuracy vs recruiter final decision   alert < 0.75
M-02 Demographic parity difference (sex, age group,
     nationality)                                     alert > 0.10
M-03 Equal-opportunity difference                     alert > 0.10
M-04 Token-distribution PSI (top-100 TF-IDF mass)     alert > 0.20
M-05 Recruiter override rate                          review > 15%
M-06 PII-leak findings per quarterly scan             alert > 0

4. REVIEW AND ESCALATION
Monthly metric review by the Model Governance Team; breaches open a
corrective action within 10 working days. Serious incidents are
reported per Article 73 within the statutory deadlines, and deployers
are notified within 48 hours.

5. FEEDBACK INTO THE RISK MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
Monitoring outcomes feed the semi-annual review of RMF-TR-v1.2-2026;
threshold breaches trigger an out-of-cycle review and, where needed, a
substantial-modification assessment under Article 25.
