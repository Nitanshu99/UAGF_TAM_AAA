AAA — Autonomous AI Auditor
===========================

An autonomous, multi-agent auditing platform for EU AI Act conformity
assessment.  A fourteen-agent pipeline ingests a provider's intake bundle,
verifies every declaration against evidence, and renders a signed audit
report with a per-article compliance matrix.
